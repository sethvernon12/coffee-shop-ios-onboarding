Coffee Shop iOS App Onboarding Guide

Individual apps under your own Apple Developer account — fully compliant with Apple’s rules (Guideline 4.2.6).

Why this process? Apple requires the content owner (your coffee shop’s legal entity) to submit the app from its own Developer account. Template-style apps submitted under an agency account are rejected under Guideline 4.2.6. This protects you and keeps the listing permanently in your name.

Step-by-Step Process

1. Obtain a free D-U-N-S Number (~5 business days)

Apple uses this nine-digit number from Dun & Bradstreet to verify your legal entity. Check if you already have one, or request a free one via Apple’s tool. Details must match exactly.

Apple D-U-N-S Lookup & Request Tool

After issuance, allow up to 2 more days for Apple to see it.

2. Enroll in the Apple Developer Program as an Organization ($99/year)

Go to developer.apple.com/programs/enroll. Select Organization. Use your legal entity name (this becomes the seller name on the App Store). Apple verifies identity and authority — total time often 2–4 weeks. Pay with a card belonging to the business so you control renewals.

3. Invite your developer as App Manager (recommended)

Once the account is active: App Store Connect → Users and Access → Invite. Use the App Manager role so we can manage the app without full financial access. Invitations expire in 3 days. You remain the Account Holder forever.

4. We build, test, and submit under your account

We handle provisioning, TestFlight, App Store submission, and responses to review. A real iPhone is needed for device testing and final submission. You own the listing from day one.

Timeline & Costs

Typical timeline: D-U-N-S (1 week) + Apple verification (1–3 weeks) = often ready in 2–4 weeks.

Cost: $99 USD per year for the Apple Developer Program (paid by you or passed through). This is the only Apple fee. Development and ongoing service are separate.

If the Relationship Ends

You can revoke our access at any time in Users and Access. The App Store listing, ratings, and account stay with you. There is no hostage situation.

Making Your App Unique

To reduce any risk under Guideline 4.3 (spam/lookalikes), we customize branding, ordering flows, loyalty features, and local content specific to your shop. Each app is submitted under its own Bundle ID from your account.

By God’s grace, this process keeps everything clean, compliant, and permanently in your name.
